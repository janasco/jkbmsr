# Plans & Pricing

JKBMSR Cloud Service is optional, and the **free tier is permanent** — a
gateway is never locked out, deleted, or cut off for not paying. There is
**no one-time license and no per-device fee**: flashing, re-flashing, and
using the platform at the free tier cost nothing.

## What changes between tiers

Every tier includes the full platform — always-on monitoring, phone alerts,
history, dashboard access, and automatic updates. The only thing a paid tier
changes is **how often your gateway relays fresh telemetry**:

| Tier | Update interval |
|---|---|
| Free | 1 hour (permanent) |
| Basic | 30 minutes |
| Lite | 15 minutes |
| Entry | 5 minutes |
| Standard | 1 minute |
| Fast | 30 seconds |

Current prices are listed on [jkbmsr.com/pricing](https://jkbmsr.com/pricing).

## Lapsing is never all-or-nothing

If a subscription lapses, isn't renewed, or is cancelled, nothing is deleted
and nothing stops — that gateway's telemetry relay simply falls back to the
free tier's 1-hour interval. Your historical telemetry stays visible and your
gateway stays claimed. Resubscribing at any time resumes the faster interval
immediately.

## Billing

Subscriptions are processed by [Polar.sh](https://polar.sh) as merchant of
record. Payments are per gateway. See the [Terms of Service](https://jkbmsr.com/terms)
and [Refund Policy](https://jkbmsr.com/refunds) for the details.
