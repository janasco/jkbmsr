# Mobile Screen List

## Login

Authenticates the customer against JKBMSR Cloud.

## Device List

Shows registered devices, online/offline status, SOC, voltage, and last seen time.

## Battery Dashboard

Shows SOC, pack voltage, current, power, temperatures, and recent device status.

## Cell Voltages

Shows per-cell voltages and highlights imbalance conditions.

## Alerts

Shows active and historical alerts.

## Settings

Shows account and device settings.

## OTA Status

Shows firmware version, update availability, and OTA status.
